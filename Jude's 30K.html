A letter from your siblings

For our brother

To our brother, Jude

Happy 30th birthday to our brother, our person, and one of the greatest gifts life has given us.

Thirty years! And somehow you are still the same Jude, with a thousand places to explore, stories to tell, miles to run, football to watch and dreams to chase.

Beyond the adventures, the travels, the running and the writing that people get to see, there is a side of you that means even more to us: the brother you are.

These past days have not been easy for our family. Losing Dad on the first has left a space that words cannot explain. Through it all, we have watched you take on so much, doing everything you can to give Dad the burial and honour he deserves. We see the effort. We see the weight you have carried. None of it goes unnoticed.

We wish Dad were here to see you turn 30. We wish we could have celebrated this milestone with him, heard his voice and seen his smile. We know this birthday may feel different, and we won't pretend everything is okay while we are still grieving.

But we also want to celebrate you.

You deserve to be celebrated too, Jude. You deserve happiness that doesn't come with responsibility, laughter that comes without effort, adventures that remind you how beautiful life can still be, and people who show up for you the way you show up for them.

We are so lucky to have you as our brother. Not only for the things you do, but because you are you. As you enter this new decade, remember that you don't always have to have everything figured out. You don't always have to be the strong one. You are allowed to rest, to feel, to dream, to ask for help, and to let other people take care of you.

May this decade bring you more places to explore, mountains to climb, finish lines to cross, stories to write, football victories to celebrate and reasons to smile. May you find success in what you are building and peace in what you cannot control.

Wherever life takes you, never forget that you have siblings who are grateful every day that you are part of our story.

Happy 30th birthday, Jude Chionyedua Enumjekonaishu. Here is to the man you are, the man you are becoming, and all the chapters still waiting for you.

We love you, big bro. Always.

Your siblings
